Slashbistro — Privacy Policy

Tan Interactive · Effective date: 26 November 2026 · Last updated: 26 November 2026 · App id com.taninteractive.slashbistro

Slashbistro ("the game") is made by Tan Interactive, a small independent game studio ("we", "us"). You can reach us at taninteractive.dev@gmail.com or taninteractive.github.io. The game is made for players aged 13 and over. This page explains what data the game and the Google services inside it handle, why, and the choices you have.

In short: no account of our own, no chat and nothing you type is collected. Your progress is saved only on your phone; we run no servers and keep no player database. Google Firebase counts game events without names or email and sends game settings. Google AdMob shows rewarded ads you choose to watch and an occasional full-screen ad after a won shift, after asking for your consent where the law requires it. The game does not request the Android Advertising ID permission and never asks for your location. We do not sell your personal information. Optional purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details, and the game keeps only the purchase receipt on your phone to deliver and restore what you bought. The Amazon Appstore version sells nothing for real money.

Data saved on your phone

  • Your progress (stars per shift, which districts and chapters are open, the story scenes you have seen, coins, gems and shards, your Kit and Helpers, kitchen upgrades, boosters, daily gifts, quests and Prize Spin, your best Dinner Rush score, whether you finished the first lesson), the purchases you made in the Google Play version (which product, and the purchase token Google Play returns, so each purchase is delivered once and one-time purchases can be restored) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language, text size and the accessibility and knife options) are kept in the app's own storage on your phone (browser storage inside the app, keys starting with slashbistro_), together with counters the ad rules need (how many ads were shown today, when the last one was shown, how many sessions you have played).
  • We have no account system and never receive this data. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include it in your Google backup under Google's terms. Otherwise uninstalling the game or clearing its storage erases it, including gems bought with real money; only one-time purchases (the Starter Pack and No Ads) can be brought back, with "Restore purchases" in Settings, because Google Play remembers that you own them.

Analytics and remote settings (Google Firebase)

  • Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) records game events so we can balance and improve the game, for example: app opened, screen viewed, shift started and finished (which shift, won or lost, stars, time, takings), tutorial steps, boosters and second chances used, Kit and Helper changes, chests opened, daily gifts, quests and spins claimed, Dinner Rush runs, Shop visits and purchases with coins or gems, settings changed, rewarded ads offered and watched, full-screen ads shown, the revenue reported for each ad shown, taps on "More games" cards, and purchases in the Google Play version (which product, its price, and whether it went through, was cancelled or failed; never card or payment details). Events contain only game ids, numbers and fixed labels, never names, email, advertising IDs or anything you type.
  • Firebase sends these events with a random app-instance ID and basic device information (model, Android version, app version, language, country or region derived from the IP address). The Android Advertising ID is not collected (the permission is removed from the app), and Analytics is told not to use the data for advertising (ad personalisation signals are off).
  • Firebase Remote Config downloads game settings (for example how often a full-screen ad may appear or how many rewarded ads a day are offered) so we can tune them without an update. It never changes the rules of a shift.
  • If the game crashes or a script error happens, an anonymous error event (where it happened, its type) is counted like any other event.

More games

  • The title screen and, now and then, the card after a won shift can show a "More games" list of other games made by Tan Interactive. To build it, the game downloads a small public file with the names, short descriptions and icons of our games from our studio website (taninteractive.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages) about once a day, and keeps a copy on your phone. The request is a plain download of the same file for every player: no personal data, game progress or identifier is sent with it, though like any web request it carries the usual technical data (such as your IP address) that GitHub, the host, handles under its own privacy policy.
  • Tapping a card opens that game's store page. You never get a reward for installing another game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Rewarded ads are always your choice: a button can double the takings of a won shift, give a second chance after a lost shift, open a free chest in the Shop, add a Prize Spin, double a daily gift, give a booster or swap a quest. Nothing happens unless you tap it, and each has a daily limit.
  • Full-screen ads between shifts only appear when you leave a won shift. Never during a shift, never after a lost shift or a second chance, never after a critic showdown, never in modes with a high score, never in your first session, never within a minute of a rewarded ad, and never when the app opens. Any purchase in the game turns them off for good. By default they start from the second shift of chapter 2, at most one every third win, at least 150 seconds apart, at most 3 an hour and 8 a day (whatever we tune later: never more often than every second win, never closer than 2 minutes, never more than 4 an hour or 12 a day). There are no banner ads.
  • Before ads load, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states). You can change your choice at any time with the "Privacy options" button in Settings (it appears where the form applies). If you do not consent, ads are not personalised; Google may still use limited data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. AdMob and its partners may use identifiers such as the app set ID and data about the ad shown (IP address, device type, ad interactions) under Google's policies. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can use Android's Privacy Sandbox ad APIs where your phone offers them and you allow them in Android's settings.
  • While this game is in closed testing, its ads are Google's test ads.

Purchases

  • On Google Play the game offers optional purchases: gem packs, a Starter Pack and No Ads. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms; we never receive your card or payment account, and Google Play keeps your purchase history.
  • The game keeps the purchase token on your phone so a purchase is delivered once and one-time purchases can be restored, and it counts purchases (product and price, no payment details) in Firebase Analytics. Any purchase turns off full-screen ads between shifts for good.
  • Gems bought with real money are stored only on your phone and are lost if you uninstall the game or clear its storage. One-time purchases (the Starter Pack and No Ads) can always be restored with "Restore purchases" in Settings.
  • Chests give a random item; the odds are shown before you open one. Coins, gems, shards and other game items cannot be sold or exchanged for real money.
  • Purchases are not available in the Amazon Appstore version.

Who receives data

  • Google Play, when you make a purchase in the Google Play version, under Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Remote Config, AdMob and its ad partners where you allow it). Google acts under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy · Privacy and security in Firebase · AdMob and user privacy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • We, Tan Interactive, only see aggregated reports and event data in the Firebase and AdMob consoles. We do not sell personal information and receive no payment details.
  • Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States.

Your rights, data retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone: uninstall the game, or open Android Settings › Apps › Slashbistro › Storage › Clear storage (Clear data on some phones). This deletes your save and settings at once, including gems bought with real money; one-time purchases can be restored later with "Restore purchases" in Settings.
  • Analytics data is tied to a random app-instance ID, not to your name or email. We have no account to look you up by. Reinstalling the game or clearing its storage creates a new ID, and the old data is no longer linked to your phone. Google Analytics deletes event data automatically after the retention period we set (up to 14 months).
  • To ask about or delete data, email taninteractive.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Slashbistro data request". We will answer within 30 days and delete whatever we can link to your installation using Google's deletion tools. Because we keep no names or accounts, we may be unable to identify data that belongs to you; in that case clearing the game's storage is the quickest way to cut the link.
  • Ad data is held by Google under its policies; you can manage it through your Google account, adssettings.google.com, Android's ad settings and the "Privacy options" button in the game.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, Swiss law or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Where the GDPR applies, analytics and remote settings rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game, and personalised ads rely on your consent. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

We keep no player data on servers of our own. Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections, and access to our Firebase and AdMob consoles is limited to the studio's own account. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we only use well-established Google services and collect as little as the game needs.

Children

Slashbistro is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not ask for your age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13: the game has no account, chat or text input, and the only data it handles are the anonymous game events, device identifiers and ad data described above. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us at taninteractive.dev@gmail.com and we will help remove any related data.
