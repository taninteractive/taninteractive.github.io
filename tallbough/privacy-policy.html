Tallbough — Privacy Policy

Tan Interactive · Effective date: 28 October 2026 · Last updated: 26 November 2026 · App id com.taninteractive.tallbough

Tallbough ("the game") is made by Tan Interactive, a small independent game studio ("we", "us"). You can reach us at taninteractive.dev@gmail.com or taninteractive.github.io. The game is made for players aged 13 and over. This page explains what data the game and the Google services inside it handle, why, and the choices you have.

In short: no account of our own, no chat and nothing you type is collected. Your progress is saved on your phone and, only if you sign in to Google Play Games (optional, Google Play version), also in your Google account's Saved Games; we run no servers and keep no player database. Google Firebase counts game events without names or email and sends game settings. Google AdMob shows rewarded ads you choose to watch and an occasional full-screen ad after a won level, after asking for your consent where the law requires it. The game does not request the Android Advertising ID permission and never asks for your location. We do not sell your personal information. Optional purchases are handled by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details, and the game keeps only the purchase receipt on your phone to deliver and restore what you bought. The Amazon Appstore version sells nothing for real money.

Data saved on your phone

  • Your progress (stars per level, which chapters are open, the friends you met and their hearts, stickers and Bark Notes you found, coins, gems and shards, gear, boosters and Heirlooms, daily rewards, whether you finished the first lesson), the purchases you made in the Google Play version (which product, and the purchase token Google Play returns, so each purchase is delivered once and one-time purchases can be restored) and your settings (sound, music, vibration, language) are kept in the app's own storage on your phone (browser storage inside the app, keys starting with tallbough_), together with counters the ad rules need (how many ads were shown today, when the last one was shown).
  • We have no account system and never receive this data. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include it in your Google backup under Google's terms. If you do not sign in to Google Play Games, uninstalling the game or clearing its storage erases it, including gems bought with real money; only one-time purchases (the Starter Pack, Ad-Free and the three Heirlooms) can be brought back, with "Restore purchases" in the Shop, because Google Play remembers that you own them. If you sign in to Google Play Games, see the next section.

Google Play Games (optional, Google Play version only)

  • The game uses Google Play Games Services to sign you in with your Play Games profile, to keep a copy of your progress in your Google account (Saved Games) and to show leaderboards. Signing in is optional: the game is fully playable without it, and it never asks you to sign in when it starts. Android signs you in automatically if you already use Play Games on the phone; you can turn this off or sign out in the Play Games app.
  • Saved Games: one saved copy of your progress (the same data as in "Data saved on your phone": stars, chapters, friends, stickers and Bark Notes, coins, gems and shards, gear and outfits, daily rewards, and a record of purchases made in the game — product, the purchase token Google Play returned and what it gave — so bought gems survive a reinstall or a new phone and are never given twice), a random id of the installation that wrote it, a short non-reversible tag of your Play Games player id (so one phone never mixes two accounts' progress), the time it changed and a short description such as "Chapter 2 · 41 ★". It does not include your settings, the ad counters, the offers shown on this phone or any year of birth an older version of the game asked for: those stay on your phone. The copy is stored by Google in your Google account under Google's terms; we cannot read it.
  • Leaderboards: your campaign stars, your best Boss Rush time and your Daily challenge time are sent to Google's public leaderboards with your Play Games name and avatar, as set in your Play Games profile. The leaderboard screen shows other players' Play Games names and avatars as Google returns them. If you turn off sharing in your Play Games profile, Google hides you from other players. The "village rivals" next to the leaderboards are characters of the game, not players.
  • Deleting it: Settings › Google Play Games › "Delete cloud progress" deletes your progress from the Saved Games copy, leaving only a small "deleted" marker with the time so your other phones do not upload their copy back without asking (the save on your phone stays, and the game stops saving to the cloud on that phone). You can also delete the game's Play Games data, including leaderboard scores, from the Play Games app or your Google account (myaccount.google.com › Data & privacy).
  • The Amazon Appstore version does not use Google Play Games.

Analytics and remote settings (Google Firebase)

  • Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) records game events so we can balance and improve the game, for example: app opened, screen viewed, level started and finished (which level, won or lost, stars, time, a game score), rewarded ads offered and watched, full-screen ads shown, the revenue reported for each ad shown, taps on "More games" cards, and purchases in the Google Play version (which product, its price and currency, and whether it went through, was cancelled or failed; never card or payment details). Events contain only game ids, numbers and fixed labels, never names, email, advertising IDs or anything you type.
  • Firebase sends these events with a random app-instance ID and basic device information (model, Android version, app version, language, country or region derived from the IP address). The Android Advertising ID is not collected (the permission is removed from the app), and Analytics is told not to use the data for advertising (ad personalisation and ad user data signals are off).
  • Firebase Remote Config downloads game settings (for example how often a full-screen ad may appear) so we can tune them without an update.
  • If the game crashes or a script error happens, an anonymous error event (where it happened, its type) is counted like any other event.

More games

  • The title screen and, now and then, the card after a won level can show a "More games" list of other games made by Tan Interactive. To build it, the game downloads a small public file with the names, short descriptions and icons of our games from our studio website (taninteractive.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages) about once a day, and keeps a copy on your phone. The request is a plain download of the same file for every player: no personal data, game progress or identifier is sent with it, though like any web request it carries the usual technical data (such as your IP address) that GitHub, the host, handles under its own privacy policy.
  • Tapping a card opens that game's store page. You never get a reward for installing another game.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Rewarded ads are always your choice: a button on the card after a won level can double the coins you earned. If we add other optional bonuses (for example a second chance after a lost level), they work the same way. Nothing happens unless you tap it.
  • Full-screen ads between levels only appear when you leave a won level. Never in the opening or the first chapter, never in your first session, never during a level, never after a lost level, never within a minute of a rewarded ad, and never when the app opens; only after 15 minutes of play. Any purchase in the game turns them off for good. By default at most one every third win, at least 150 seconds apart, at most 3 an hour and 8 a day (whatever we tune later: never before 10 minutes of play, never more often than every second win, never closer than 2 minutes, never more than 4 an hour or 12 a day). There are no banner ads.
  • Before ads load, the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and some US states). You can change your choice at any time with the "Ad privacy choices" button on the title screen (it appears where the form applies). If you do not consent, ads are not personalised; Google may still use limited data to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for teens. AdMob and its partners may use identifiers such as the app set ID and data about the ad shown (IP address, device type, ad interactions) under Google's policies. The Google Mobile Ads SDK can use Android's Privacy Sandbox ad APIs where your phone offers them and you allow them in Android's settings.
  • While this game is in closed testing, its ads are Google's test ads.

Purchases

  • On Google Play the game offers optional purchases: gem packs, a Starter Pack, Ad-Free and three Heirlooms. Payment is handled entirely by Google Play under Google's terms; we never receive your card or payment account, and Google Play keeps your purchase history.
  • The game keeps the purchase token on your phone so a purchase is delivered once and one-time purchases can be restored, and it counts purchases (product and price, no payment details) in Firebase Analytics. Any purchase turns off full-screen ads between levels for good.
  • Gems bought with real money are stored on your phone and, if you sign in to Google Play Games, in your Saved Games copy, so they come back on a reinstall or a new phone once you sign in; without signing in they are lost if you uninstall the game or clear its storage. One-time purchases (the Starter Pack, Ad-Free and the Heirlooms) can always be restored with "Restore purchases" in the Shop.
  • Gold chests can be opened with gems and give a random item; the odds are shown on the chest screen. Coins, gems and other game items cannot be sold or exchanged for real money.
  • Purchases are not available in the Amazon Appstore version.

Who receives data

  • Google Play, when you make a purchase in the Google Play version, under Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • Google Play Games, if you sign in: your Saved Games copy and leaderboard scores, under Google's terms and privacy policy.
  • Google (Firebase Analytics, Firebase Remote Config, AdMob and its ad partners where you allow it). Google acts under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy · Privacy and security in Firebase · AdMob and user privacy · How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.
  • We, Tan Interactive, only see aggregated reports and event data in the Firebase and AdMob consoles. We do not sell personal information and receive no payment details.
  • Data sent to Google is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States.

Your rights, data retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone: uninstall the game, or open Android Settings › Apps › Tallbough › Storage › Clear storage (Clear data on some phones). This deletes your save and settings at once, including gems bought with real money unless you signed in to Google Play Games; one-time purchases can be restored later with "Restore purchases" in the Shop.
  • Cloud copy (Google Play Games): Settings › Google Play Games › "Delete cloud progress", or delete the game's data in the Play Games app / your Google account. Leaderboard scores are removed by Google when you delete the game's Play Games data.
  • Analytics data is tied to a random app-instance ID, not to your name or email. We have no account to look you up by. Reinstalling the game or clearing its storage creates a new ID, and the old data is no longer linked to your phone. Google Analytics deletes event data automatically after the retention period we set (up to 14 months).
  • To ask about or delete data, email taninteractive.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Tallbough data request". We will answer within 30 days and delete whatever we can link to your installation using Google's deletion tools. Because we keep no names or accounts, we may be unable to identify data that belongs to you; in that case clearing the game's storage is the quickest way to cut the link.
  • Ad data is held by Google under its policies; you can manage it through your Google account, adssettings.google.com, Android's ad settings and the "Ad privacy choices" button in the game.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, Swiss law or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Where the GDPR applies, analytics and remote settings rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game, and personalised ads rely on your consent. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

We keep no player data on servers of our own. Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections, and access to our Firebase and AdMob consoles is limited to the studio's own account. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we only use well-established Google services and collect as little as the game needs.

Children

Tallbough is made for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. The game does not ask for your age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13: the game has no account, chat or text input, and the only data it handles are the anonymous game events, device identifiers and ad data described above. Earlier test versions asked once for a year of birth; if your phone still holds that answer from such a version, the game no longer uses it and it stays on your phone. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us at taninteractive.dev@gmail.com and we will help remove any related data.
