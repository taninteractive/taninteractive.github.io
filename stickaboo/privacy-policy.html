Stickaboo — Privacy Policy

Tan Interactive · Effective date: 23 October 2026 · Last updated: 23 October 2026 · App id com.taninteractive.stickaboo

Stickaboo ("the game") is made by Tan Interactive, a small independent game studio ("we", "us"). You can reach us at taninteractive.dev@gmail.com or taninteractive.github.io. The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. This page explains what data the game and the Google services inside it handle, why, and the choices you have. It covers both the Google Play version and the Amazon Appstore version.

In short: no account, no sign-in, no chat and nothing you type is collected. Your progress and purchases are saved only on your phone; we run no servers and keep no player database. Google Firebase counts game events without names or email and sends game settings. Google AdMob shows rewarded ads you choose to watch and an occasional full-screen ad after a won page, after asking for your consent where the law requires it. The game does not request the Android Advertising ID permission and never asks for your location. We do not sell your personal information. Purchases go through Google Play; the Amazon Appstore version sells nothing for real money.

What the game stores on your phone

  • Your save: album pages cleared, stars and page scores, Coins, Gems and Offcuts, your Craft Box tools and upgrades, Holo stickers, boosters and revive tickets, chests (Envelopes and Gift Boxes), the Sticker Calendar and Shiny Streak, daily tasks, the Album Pass, the Star Card, the Piggy bank, the Sticker Shelf, badges, the Fusion Book and Zombie Book, Daily challenge and event records, simple play counters, and a record of what you have bought and received.
  • Your settings: sound, music, haptics, language, text size, reduce motion, high contrast, quiet zombies, tap to place, bigger fusion magnet, relaxed speed, whether you have finished the tutorial, and your answers to one-time notes.
  • All of this is kept in the app's own storage on your phone (browser storage inside the app, keys starting with stickaboo_). There is no account and no cloud save, and we never receive it. If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include it in your Google backup under Google's terms.
  • Uninstalling the game or clearing its storage erases it. On the Google Play version, Restore purchases (in Settings) brings back one-time purchases: Holo stickers, No Ads, Heart Sticker Skins, the Border Collection and outfit packs. Items used up when delivered, such as Gems, boosters, revive tickets and the contents of packs, bundles and offers, the Star Card, the Piggy bank and the Album Pass, exist only in the save on your phone and are lost if you uninstall the game. The game tells you this once in the Shop.
  • The game has no account, no sign-in, no names, no email, no chat, no free-text input, no online leaderboards (Daily challenge and event records stay on your phone), no trading and no user-generated content. It does not ask for access to your contacts, photos, files, camera, microphone or location.

Analytics (Google Firebase Analytics)

  • Firebase Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase) records game events so we can balance and improve the game, for example: app opened, screen viewed, page started and finished (won or lost, stars, score, time, Heart left, fusions, clean drops, boosters and revives used), tutorial steps, the stickers picked for a page, in-game currency earned and spent, chests opened, upgrades, daily rewards and tasks claimed, rewarded ads offered and watched, full-screen ads shown, the revenue reported for each ad shown, and purchase steps in the Shop. Events contain only game ids, numbers and fixed labels, never names, email, advertising IDs, purchase tokens or anything you type.
  • Firebase also collects some data automatically: an app-instance ID (a random identifier created for this installation), the device model, Android version, app version, language, and an approximate location (such as country or region) that Google derives from your IP address. It does not collect your precise location.
  • Analytics is not used for advertising. The game turns Firebase's advertising signals (ad storage, ad user data and ad personalisation) off by default for every player, and it does not request the Android Advertising ID permission.
  • Error reports: if the game hits an unexpected error it sends one analytics event (app_error, at most 5 per session) with only the place in the game's code and the kind of error. There is no crash-reporting SDK; crash statistics, if any, come from Android itself (Android vitals in Google Play) under Google's policies.
  • We set Google Analytics to keep event data for up to 14 months; after that Google deletes it automatically.

Remote settings (Google Firebase Remote Config)

  • Firebase Remote Config downloads game settings, for example reward sizes, prices in Coins and Gems, how often full-screen ads may appear and daily ad limits, so we can tune the game without an update. Every value is limited to a safe range inside the game; it can never change the rules of a page, make chest odds worse than the odds shown in the game, raise ad limits above the ones on this page, or be set differently for you than for other players.
  • To do this, Firebase uses a Firebase installation ID (a random identifier for this installation) and the usual technical data of a network request, such as your IP address.

More games

  • The title screen can show a "More games" button that lists other games made by Tan Interactive, and a won page may show one small card for one of them. To build this list, the game downloads a small public file with the names, short descriptions and icons of our games from our studio website (taninteractive.github.io, hosted on GitHub Pages) about once a day, and keeps a copy on your phone. The request is a plain download of the same file for every player: no personal data, game progress or identifier is sent with it, though like any web request it carries the usual technical data (such as your IP address) that GitHub, the host, handles under its own privacy policy.
  • Tapping "View" only opens that game's page in the store app (Google Play or the Amazon Appstore) or in your browser. Nothing is installed automatically, and installing another game never gives you a reward in this game.
  • Firebase Analytics counts when a suggestion is shown or tapped (promo_view, promo_click: which suggested game and where), like any other game event.

Ads (Google AdMob)

  • Rewarded ads are always your choice. You tap a button to watch one, for example to double your Coins after a win, keep playing after a lost page ("Stick Around!"), get a free booster for a page, open an extra Envelope, spin the Daily spin again, collect the Sticker Shelf twice, refresh the Daily deals, swap a daily task, double today's Sticker Calendar reward, double an event reward or try a Holo sticker for one page. Each has its own daily limit and by default there are never more than 20 rewarded ads a day in total. The button only appears when an ad is ready, and if an ad fails after you tap, you still get the reward.
  • Full-screen ads between pages only appear when you leave a won page. Never in your first session, never during a page, never after a lost page, never right after a revive or within a minute of a rewarded ad, never before a story scene and never when the app opens; only after page 1-10 is cleared and after 15 minutes of play. By default at most one every third win, at least 3 minutes apart, at most 6 a session and 10 a day (whatever we tune later: never before 10 minutes of play, never more often than every second win, never closer than 2 minutes, never more than 8 a session or 12 a day). Any purchase in the game turns them off for good.
  • There are no banner ads and no ads when the app opens. Ad content is limited to the PG rating (parental guidance) or lower.
  • Advertising identifiers: the game does not request the Android Advertising ID permission (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID is removed from the app), so AdMob cannot read your device's Advertising ID. AdMob may still use other device signals, such as the app set ID, your IP address (for approximate location), device and app information, and ad interaction and diagnostic data, to show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. On Android versions that offer it, the Google Mobile Ads SDK declares the Android Privacy Sandbox ad services (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS), which Google may use as described in its own documentation; you control them in your device's privacy settings.
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. AdMob and user privacy: support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543.
  • The Amazon Appstore version shows the same AdMob ads under the same rules.

Your consent choices

  • Before any ad is requested, the game checks with Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), Google's consent message is shown first. You can consent, refuse ("Do not consent") or choose purpose by purpose ("Manage options"). If you refuse, the game still plays in full, with limited, non-personalised ads.
  • In US states with consumer privacy laws, Google's message lets you opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising.
  • You can change your choice at any time: Settings › Ad privacy choices (the row appears wherever your region has such a form). Settings › Privacy policy opens this page.
  • You can also reset or delete your device's advertising identifiers and limit ad personalisation in Android's own settings (Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads, depending on the device), and manage personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

Purchases

  • Google Play version: optional purchases are made through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The purchase token Google Play returns is checked on your phone to deliver the item and to restore one-time purchases; we run no purchase server. Google Play keeps your purchase history, and Firebase Analytics records each purchase (which item, its price and currency) like any other game event.
  • Amazon Appstore version: sells nothing for real money. Its Shop has no real-money items; Holo stickers are bought with Gems earned in the game, and the Album Pass has its free track only.
  • No paid random items. Real money and Gems never buy a random reward. Envelopes (the free chest) are free every few hours or come from a rewarded ad, and Gift Boxes cost Coins, which are only earned by playing and are never sold or included in any paid item. Pick-a-Pack lets you choose exactly what you get. Odds and pity counters are shown on every chest button before you open one. Spins of the Daily spin are free or come from a rewarded ad and are never sold. Offers have no countdowns.

Your rights, data retention and deletion

  • Data on your phone: uninstall the game, or open Android Settings › Apps › Stickaboo › Storage › Clear storage (Clear data on some phones). This deletes your save and settings at once.
  • Analytics data is tied to a random app-instance ID, not to your name or email. We have no account to look you up by. Reinstalling the game or clearing its storage creates a new ID, and the old data is no longer linked to your phone. Google Analytics deletes event data automatically after the retention period we set (up to 14 months).
  • To ask about or delete data, email taninteractive.dev@gmail.com with the subject "Stickaboo data request". We will answer within 30 days and delete whatever we can link to your installation using Google's deletion tools. Because we keep no names or accounts, we may be unable to identify data that belongs to you; in that case clearing the game's storage is the quickest way to cut the link.
  • Ad data is held by Google under its policies; you can manage it through your Google account, adssettings.google.com, Android's ad settings and Settings › Ad privacy choices in the game.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, Swiss law or US state laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. Where the GDPR applies, analytics and remote settings rely on our legitimate interest in running and improving the game, and personalised ads rely on your consent. If you are in the EEA, the UK or Switzerland, you may also complain to your data protection authority.

Security

We keep no player data on servers of our own. Data sent to Google services travels over encrypted connections, and access to our Firebase and AdMob consoles is limited to the studio's own account. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but we only use well-established Google services and collect as little as the game needs.

Children

Stickaboo is intended for players aged 13 and over (a general audience). It is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme, is not directed at children under 13 and does not ask for your age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game, contact us and we will help remove any related data. If we ever open the game to younger players, we will update this page first, and players under 13 would then only get non-personalised, child-directed ads.

This page and the game link to Google's own pages (privacy policy, Firebase, AdMob, ad settings) and to store pages of our other games. Those services are run by Google, Amazon and GitHub under their own privacy policies, which we do not control.
